    Get to know who about me

Hi, I’m Kiplimo

I come from Eldoret, Kenya, and I grew up in a humble background. Today I live and work in Doha, Qatar 🇶🇦, where I’m a sales consultant at a consultancy. Every day I speak with people who are making big decisions about their careers and their futures, and that has taught me how much trust, patience and clear communication matter.

My story

Coming from where I come from taught me that nothing arrives for free. You learn, you work, and you keep going, even when the month is hard. I started Kiplimo Global to do that in the open: to learn sales in public, share the lessons from my targets, my cancellations and my wins, and grow my skills day by day.

What I believe

-Sales is helping someone solve a problem and getting paid for it.
-Trust and honesty close more deals than pressure.
-Consistency beats motivation.
-Skills are the one asset nobody can take from you.

What I share;

-Closing and follow-ups
-Pricing and handling objections
-Sales mindset and discipline
-Money and business basics

Where I’m going

I’m here to keep learning, developing and growing. My plan is to build my skills in sales and business, grow a brand people trust, and explore real estate and my own business ventures along the way. Kiplimo Global is where that journey happens.
