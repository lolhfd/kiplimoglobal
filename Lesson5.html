  • Ditch Liabilities That Drain Your Cash

    Reduce unnecessary costs and debt so more of your money can support business growth

Section I

Liabilities can quietly limit your choices by creating regular payments without adding enough value in return. Review subscriptions, loans, equipment leases, and other ongoing expenses.

Ask whether each one helps you earn, operate, or serve customers.

Section II

Cancel unused services, renegotiate costly contracts, and make a realistic plan to pay down high-cost debt. Avoid taking on new obligations simply to look successful or keep up with others.

Some expenses are necessary, so consider their purpose and impact before making changes.

The aim is to free up cash and reduce financial pressure, giving your business more room to handle unexpected costs and invest in useful opportunities.
