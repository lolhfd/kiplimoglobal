  • Buy Assets That Build Value

    Put money into resources that can support your work, generate income, or grow in value over time.

Section I

Before buying something for your business, consider whether it helps you earn, save time, or serve clients better.

Useful assets might include reliable equipment, software, training, or tools that improve your service.

Section II

Compare the cost with the value you expect to receive, and avoid purchases that strain your cash flow or are driven mainly by status.

Keep personal spending separate from business investment, and track what each purchase contributes. Not every asset produces immediate income, so review your choices over time.

Thoughtful buying can strengthen your business; buying without a clear purpose can tie up money you need elsewhere.
